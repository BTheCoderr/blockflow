Privacy

Updated October 1, 2026

No account required

Block Flow does not ask you to create an account or provide a name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone access, or camera access.

Game data stays on your device

Level progress, best moves, best times, settings, saved puzzle state, and playtest aggregates are stored in your browser's local storage. Block Flow does not send those gameplay records to an application server.

Playtest report

The Copy playtest report control only copies local aggregate gameplay data to your clipboard when you tap it. Nothing is transmitted automatically.

Hosting

The hosting provider may process standard web request information needed to deliver the site, such as IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, and timestamps, under its own infrastructure policies.

Analytics and advertising

The current Block Flow build does not include third-party advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics scripts.
